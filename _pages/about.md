Hi! I am Siying Chen, a **Research Assistant** at **Duke University**, working in **[DexLab](https://robotdexteritylab.com/)** with **[Prof. Xianyi Cheng](https://xianyicheng.github.io/)** on contact-rich robotic manipulation. Before this, I earned my M.S. in ECE at Duke University and my B.S. in Computer Science at Macau University of Science and Technology. I am particularly interested in contact-rich manipulation, sim-to-real transfer, multimodal perception, and task-relevant representation learning. My long-term goal is to build robots that understand, adapt to, and interact with the physical world using the right information. My research is driven by questions such as:

1. **Physical interaction:** How can robots understand and act through contact as naturally as humans do?
2. **Simulation and reality:** What does it take for skills learned in simulation to work in the real world?
3. **Information:** What information does a robot actually need to act well, and how can it learn to find it on its own?
4. **Perception:** How can robots combine vision, touch, and force into one coherent understanding of the world?
{: .research-questions}
